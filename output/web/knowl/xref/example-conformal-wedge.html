Example 4.5.3 Source in a wedge.

Fluid is contained in a wedge with walls at \(\theta = 0\) and \(\theta = \alpha > 0\text{,}\) with the fluid in \(0 < \theta < \alpha\text{.}\) Find the complex potential of a source of strength \(Q\) at the point \(z = c\) in the wedge.
🔗
Solution.
Consider the map
\begin{equation} \zeta = g(z) = z^{\pi/\alpha}.\tag{4.5.1} \end{equation}
With \(z = r\e^{\im\theta}\text{,}\) we have \(\zeta = r^{\pi/\alpha}\e^{\im\pi\theta/\alpha}\text{,}\) so \(0 < \theta < \alpha\) is mapped to \(0 < \arg\zeta < \pi\text{,}\) the upper half-\(\zeta\)-plane. The ray \(\theta = 0\) is mapped to the positive real axis and the ray \(\theta = \alpha\) to the negative real axis, as shown in Figure 4.13.
🔗
In the \(\zeta\)-plane, we must solve for flow in the upper half-plane with a source of strength \(Q\) at \(\zeta_c = c^{\pi/\alpha}\text{,}\) and an impermeable boundary on the real \(\zeta\)-axis. By the method of images (Section 4.4), we place sources at both \(\zeta_c\) and its complex conjugate, \(\overline{\zeta_c}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation} F(\zeta) = \frac{Q}{2\pi} \log(\zeta - \zeta_c) + \frac{Q}{2\pi} \log(\zeta - \overline{\zeta_c}).\tag{4.5.2} \end{equation}
Composing with the map, the complex potential in the \(z\)-plane is
\begin{equation} f(z) = F(g(z)) = \frac{Q}{2\pi} \log(z^{\pi/\alpha} - c^{\pi/\alpha}) + \frac{Q}{2\pi} \log(z^{\pi/\alpha} - \overline{c^{\pi/\alpha}}).\tag{4.5.3} \end{equation}
By construction (Proposition 4.5.2), the walls of the wedge are streamlines.
