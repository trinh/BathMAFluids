Exercise 9. Decomposition of a shear flow.

Based on Exercise 6.8 in [4].
🔗
In this problem, you will apply the equation from the previous problem Exercise 6.9.8 to a shear flow \(\bu=(\beta y,0,0)\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Use (6.9.1) to decompose \(\bu\) into its local (i) translational, (ii) rotational, and (iii) pure straining components. For (iii), find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors, which give the principle directions of strain. Sketch each of the components (ii) and (iii).
🔗
Note that this example is a special case in which the rotational and pure straining components are uniform over the fluid domain. More generally, both will vary.
