Example 4.3.6 Uniform flow.

Show that the complex potential of the uniform flow of Example 4.1.2 is \(f(z) = U \e^{-\im \alpha} z\text{,}\) and recover \(\phi\text{,}\) \(\psi\) and \(\bu\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
With \(z = x + \im y\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} U\e^{-\im\alpha}z = U(x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha) + \im U(y\cos\alpha - x\sin\alpha), \end{equation*}
whose real and imaginary parts are the \(\phi\) and \(\psi\) of Example 4.1.2 and the corresponding example in Section 4.2. Also \(f'(z) = U\e^{-\im\alpha} = U\cos\alpha - \im U\sin\alpha\text{,}\) so \(\bu = U[\cos\alpha, \sin\alpha]\text{.}\)
