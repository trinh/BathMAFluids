Theorem 3.5.8 Bernoulli’s equation for steady irrotational flow.

For a steady and irrotational flow,
\begin{equation} B(\bx) = \frac{p}{\rho} + \frac{1}{2} |\bu|^2 + \chi = \textrm{constant everywhere}.\tag{3.5.8} \end{equation}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For steady flow, the first term of (3.5.5) vanishes. If, in addition, the flow is irrotational, then \(\bomega = \mathbf{0}\text{,}\) and therefore
\begin{equation*} \nabla B = \mathbf{0}. \end{equation*}
Since all spatial derivatives of \(B\) are zero, \(B\) is constant throughout the (connected) fluid domain.
