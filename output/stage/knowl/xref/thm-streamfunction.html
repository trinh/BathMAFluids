Theorem 4.2.1 The streamfunction is constant along streamlines.

The streamfunction, \(\psi(x, y, t)\text{,}\) is constant along streamlines of the flow (i.e. curves that are everywhere tangent to \(\bu\) at a fixed time \(t\)).
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The proof follows simply by the fact that
\begin{equation*} \bu \cdot \nabla \psi = \left(\pd{\psi}{y}, -\pd{\psi}{x}\right) \cdot \left(\pd{\psi}{x}, \pd{\psi}{y}\right) = 0, \end{equation*}
where the first equality uses (4.2.2).
🔗
Since \(\nabla\psi\) is normal to the level sets of \(\psi\text{,}\) and \(\bu\) is orthogonal to \(\nabla\psi\text{,}\) the velocity \(\bu\) is tangent to the level sets. These level sets are therefore streamlines.
