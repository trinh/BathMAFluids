Example 4.3.7 Stagnation point flow.

Show that the complex potential of the stagnation point flow of Example 4.1.3 is \(f(z) = z^2/2\text{,}\) and recover \(\phi\text{,}\) \(\psi\) and \(\bu\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
We have \(z^2/2 = \frac12(x^2 - y^2) + \im xy\text{,}\) so \(\phi = \frac12(x^2 - y^2)\) and \(\psi = xy\text{.}\) Also \(f'(z) = z = x + \im y = u - \im v\text{,}\) so \(\bu = [x, -y]\text{.}\)
