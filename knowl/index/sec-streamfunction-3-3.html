Example 4.2.5 Uniform flow.

Find the streamfunction \(\psi\) for the uniform flow of Example 4.1.2, and sketch its streamlines.
🔗
Solution.
Integrating \(u = \psi_y = U\cos\alpha\) and \(v = -\psi_x = U\sin\alpha\) gives, up to an arbitrary constant,
\begin{equation*} \psi = Uy\cos\alpha - Ux\sin\alpha. \end{equation*}
Lines of constant \(\psi\) are \(y = x\tan\alpha + \textrm{constant}\text{,}\) as in Figure 4.3.
