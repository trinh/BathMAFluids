Example 4.3.9 Line vortex flow.

Show that the complex potential of the line vortex of Example 4.2.9 is
\begin{equation} f(z) = -\frac{\im \Gamma}{2\pi} \log z,\tag{4.3.7} \end{equation}
and recover \(\phi\text{,}\) \(\psi\) and \(\bu\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Using (4.3.6) with \(z = r\e^{\im\theta}\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} f(z) = \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi} (\theta - \im \log r), \end{equation*}
so \(\phi = \Gamma\theta/(2\pi)\) and \(\psi = -(\Gamma/2\pi) \log r\text{,}\) which is constant on circles \(r = \textrm{constant}\text{.}\) The velocity follows from
\begin{equation*} u - \im v = -\frac{\im\Gamma}{2\pi z} = -\frac{\im\Gamma}{2\pi r}\e^{-\im\theta}, \end{equation*}
so \(u + \im v = \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi r}\im\e^{\im\theta}\text{,}\) i.e. \(\bu = \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi r}\be_\theta\text{,}\) as in (4.2.9).
