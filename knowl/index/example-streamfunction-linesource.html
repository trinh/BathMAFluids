Example 4.2.7 Line source.

Find the streamfunction \(\psi\) for the line source of Example 4.1.4, and sketch its streamlines.
🔗
Solution.
From (G.1.30), the velocity is entirely radial,
\begin{equation} \bu = \pd{\phi}{r}\be_r = \frac{Q}{2\pi r} \be_r.\tag{4.2.7} \end{equation}
Using (4.2.6), we integrate \(\frac{1}{r} \pd{\psi}{\theta} = \frac{Q}{2\pi r}\) (with \(\pd{\psi}{r} = -u_\theta = 0\)) to give
\begin{equation*} \psi = \frac{Q}{2\pi} \theta. \end{equation*}
The lines of constant \(\psi\) are the radial lines of constant \(\theta\text{,}\) as in Figure 4.5.
