Example 6.2.7 Droplet pressure.

A spherical fluid droplet of radius \(R\) has coefficient of surface tension \(\gamma\text{.}\) What is the pressure difference between the inside and outside of the droplet?
\begin{equation*} \Delta p = p_{\textrm{in}} - p_{\textrm{out}} = \frac{2\gamma}{R}. \end{equation*}
