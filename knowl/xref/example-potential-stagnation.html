Example 4.1.3 Stagnation point flow.

Show that \(\phi = \frac{1}{2} (x^2 - y^2)\) is the velocity potential of an incompressible flow, and find the velocity \(\bu\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Since \(\pd{^2\phi}{x^2} + \pd{^2\phi}{y^2} = 1 - 1 = 0\text{,}\) Laplace’s equation is satisfied. The velocity is
\begin{equation*} \bu = \nabla\phi = [x, -y], \end{equation*}
which vanishes at the origin. This is stagnation point flow, shown below.
