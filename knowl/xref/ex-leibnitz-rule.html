Exercise 3. Leibniz’s rule.

The Reynolds’ Transport Theorem Theorem 3.1.3, which relates to the passage of a time derivative through an integral, is a general form of the 1D Leibniz rule:
\begin{align} \dd{}{t} & \int_{a(t)}^{b(t)} f(x, t) \, \de{x} \notag\\ & = \int_{a(t)}^{b(t)} \pd{f}{t} \, \de{x} + f(b(t), t) \dot{b}(t) - f(a(t), t)\dot{a}(t).\tag{3.6.1} \end{align}
