Exercise 5. A source in a semi-infinite flow.

Consider the situation of two point sources of identical strength, \(Q\text{,}\) placed at \(z = \pm d\text{,}\) with \(d \gt 0\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Develop equations for the complex potential, \(f(z) = \phi + \im \psi\text{,}\) and complex velocity, \(u - \im v\text{.}\)
🔗
Note: the algebra quickly becomes awkward, whatever form you use. In this part, you are not expected to simplify beyond writing the streamfunction in a form such as \(\psi = \frac{Q}{2\pi}\operatorname{Arg}\big[(x + \im y)^2 - d^2\big]\text{.}\) You may then confirm the shape of the streamlines, \(\psi = \textrm{constant}\text{,}\) with a plot.
🔗
🔗

(b)

Let \(\beta\) denote the constant value of \(\operatorname{Arg}(z^2 - d^2)\) along a given streamline. Show that, on this streamline,
\begin{equation*} \frac{2xy}{x^2 - y^2 - d^2} = \tan\beta. \end{equation*}
🔗
Hint.
Start from part (a): explain why \(\psi = \textrm{constant}\) means that \(\operatorname{Arg}(z^2 - d^2)\) is constant along a streamline.
🔗
Next, substitute \(z = x + \im y\) and expand \(z^2 - d^2\) to find its real and imaginary parts.
🔗
Finally, recall that any complex number can be written as \(w = a + \im b = |w|\e^{\im\beta}\text{,}\) where \(\beta = \operatorname{Arg} w\text{.}\) Comparing real and imaginary parts gives \(a = |w|\cos\beta\) and \(b = |w|\sin\beta\text{,}\) so that \(b/a = \tan\beta\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Hence show that the streamlines are hyperbolae, and find their equation.
🔗
Hint.
Multiply out the result of part (b), writing \(k = \cot\beta\text{,}\) and rearrange it into the form \(Ax^2 + Bxy + Cy^2 = D\text{.}\) Recall that such a conic is a hyperbola when \(B^2 - 4AC \gt 0\text{.}\)
