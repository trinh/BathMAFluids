Example 4.6.2 Uniform flow past a circular cylinder.

Use the Circle Theorem to find the complex potential for uniform horizontal flow of speed \(U\) past a circular cylinder of radius \(a\text{,}\) centred at the origin.
🔗
Solution.
We begin with the complex potential \(f(z) = Uz\text{,}\) which corresponds to horizontal uniform flow. It is non-singular for all finite \(z\text{,}\) so it satisfies the requirements of the Circle Theorem. Hence
\begin{equation} w(z) = \phi + \im \psi = Uz + \overline{\frac{Ua^2}{\overline{z}}} = Uz + \frac{Ua^2}{z}.\tag{4.6.2} \end{equation}
