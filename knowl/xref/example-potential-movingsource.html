Example 4.1.5 Line source in a uniform flow.

Find the velocity potential \(\phi\) and the velocity \(\bu\) of a line source of strength \(Q\) at the origin placed in a uniform flow of speed \(U\) in the \(x\)-direction.
🔗
Solution.
Adding the potentials of Example 4.1.2 (with \(\alpha = 0\)) and Example 4.1.4,
\begin{equation*} \phi = Ux + \frac{Q}{2\pi} \log r = Ux + \frac{Q}{2\pi} \log \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}, \end{equation*}
and so
\begin{equation*} \bu = [U, 0] + \frac{Q}{2\pi(x^2 + y^2)} [x, y]. \end{equation*}
