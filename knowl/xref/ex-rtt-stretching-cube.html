This exercise completes Example 3.1.5. Consider the steady velocity field \(\bu = \left(x, \; -\tfrac12 y, \; 0\right)\text{,}\) and let \(V(t)\) be the material volume that initially occupies the unit cube \(V(0) = [0,1]^3\text{.}\) From the example, \(V(t) = [0, \, \e^{t}] \times [0, \, \e^{-t/2}] \times [0, \, 1]\) and the Jacobian is \(J = \e^{t/2}\text{.}\) Let
\begin{equation*} f(\bx) = x^2 + y, \qquad I(t) = \iiint_{V(t)} f \, \de{x} \, \de{y} \, \de{z}. \end{equation*}
🔗

(a)

By integrating directly over \(V(t)\text{,}\) compute \(I(t)\) and hence \(\de{I}/\de{t}\text{.}\)
