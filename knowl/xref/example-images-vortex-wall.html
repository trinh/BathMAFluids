Example 4.4.3 Line vortex near a wall.

Find the complex potential of a line vortex of strength \(\Gamma\) at \(z = d\) in the half-plane \(x \gt 0\text{,}\) bounded by a wall at \(x = 0\text{,}\) and verify the no-flux condition on the wall.
🔗
Solution.
Near \(z = d\text{,}\) we expect \(f(z) \sim -\frac{\im\Gamma}{2\pi} \log(z - d)\text{,}\) but this potential alone does not satisfy the no-flux condition at \(x = 0\text{.}\) We add an image vortex at \(z = -d\) of opposite sign:
\begin{equation*} f(z) = -\frac{\im\Gamma}{2\pi} \log(z - d) + \frac{\im\Gamma}{2\pi} \log(z + d). \end{equation*}
For \(\Gamma \gt 0\text{,}\) this flow is composed of a line vortex circulating anticlockwise on the right, and a line vortex circulating clockwise on the left. The complex velocity is
\begin{equation*} u - \im v = -\frac{\im \Gamma d}{\pi(z^2 - d^2)}, \end{equation*}
and on \(z = \im y\text{,}\) \(u - \im v = \im\Gamma d/[\pi(y^2 + d^2)]\text{,}\) so \(u = 0\) and there is no flux through the wall.
